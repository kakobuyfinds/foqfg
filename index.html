03 / Compare with context

Fit, photos and the full cost.

A practical sizing and QC checklist

Clothing: compare chest width, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length with a garment you already wear. Check whether a chart shows flat garment measurements or body measurements.

Footwear: record the size system and the seller’s measurement guidance. A familiar size label alone is not enough to compare fit across different listings.

Received-item photos: check color, quantity, size labels, visible construction and any requested measurements. Ask for a close-up when a detail cannot be judged from the available angle.

Photographs can reveal visible differences, but they cannot establish comfort, durability, material composition or authenticity by themselves.

04 / Useful answers

JoyaGoo spreadsheet FAQ

What is this JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is an independent product discovery page with selected CAFANS listings and tools for building a shopping shortlist. It is not an official JoyaGoo catalog, checkout or inventory service.

Where do the product links and images come from?

The six catalog entries were taken from the CAFANS homepage on 19 November 2026. Each product link opens its corresponding CAFANS detail page. The main JoyaGoo spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons both open the CAFANS homepage.

Can I buy every listed item through JoyaGoo?

Compatibility has not been verified for these entries. Check the CAFANS product details for the original seller link, then confirm the exact listing and ordering eligibility with your chosen agent before paying.

Do the USD prices include shipping?

No complete delivered price is provided here. The displayed values are CAFANS item-price estimates from the catalog snapshot. Check current item pricing and add applicable delivery charges, service fees, international shipping and taxes separately.

Are the product photos QC photos?

No. These are catalog photographs from the source listings. Warehouse QC photos, when available, should show the actual item received for your order. Match those photos against the exact color, size and version you selected.

How does the shortlist download work?

Select Save on any product, then choose Download shortlist CSV. The file includes product references, source links, snapshot prices and blank columns for your own sizing and QC notes. The shortlist stays in this browser tab’s memory and resets when the page reloads.

What should I do with an unavailable or changed listing?

Mark the saved row as unavailable or changed and check the current source before looking for alternatives. Do not treat a matching photograph as proof that another listing has the same seller, specifications or purchase terms.
